Our Story

A small dairy counter built on an old promise

Anantjeet Dairy began as a single milk counter, started with one simple idea — that a neighbourhood deserves milk and dairy it can trust without question. What started as a small daily round has grown into a full range of dairy products, but the way we source, handle, and deliver them hasn't changed.

Every batch is still checked by hand, every delivery still timed for the morning, and every customer still treated like a neighbour — because that's exactly what they are.

Our Mission

Fresh, honest dairy — delivered without shortcuts

Our mission is simple: bring fresh, quality dairy products to every household we serve, at a fair price, without compromising on hygiene or taste. We source close to home, process in small batches, and get products to our customers as quickly as possible so freshness is never in question.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted dairy name in our community

We want every household nearby to think of Anantjeet Dairy first when it comes to milk, paneer, ghee, and curd — not because we're the only option, but because we've earned that trust delivery after delivery. As we grow, our vision is to keep that same neighbourhood feel, even as we reach more homes.
